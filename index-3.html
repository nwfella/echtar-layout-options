Our Story

From Trading Roots to Global Solutions

Echtar Group (EG) began as a focused trading and distribution company in 1983. Over four decades, we have transformed into a comprehensive business solutions provider — bridging complex engineering theory with practical reality.

Today we operate across Europe, the Middle East, and Ukraine, delivering end-to-end lifecycle support for infrastructure, energy, and industrial projects. Our team of over 40 highly qualified professionals brings together deep domain expertise with a "Smart Design" philosophy.

"We partner with our clients — not just as contractors, but as long-term strategic allies. Every project we undertake is built on mutual trust, rigorous governance, and uncompromising HSSE standards." — Echtar Group, Leadership
